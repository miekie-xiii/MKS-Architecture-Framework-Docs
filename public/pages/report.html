Report an Issue

If you encounter a bug, unexpected behavior, or a possible exploit while using the MKS Architecture Framework, please report it through the official Discord server.

Providing clear and detailed information helps make the issue easier to identify, reproduce, and investigate.

Report a Bug

When reporting a bug, describe what happened, what you expected to happen, and the steps required to reproduce the issue.

Report an Exploit

If you discover a possible exploit, report it responsibly through the official Discord server. Provide enough information for the issue to be investigated, but avoid publicly sharing details that could allow others to abuse the vulnerability.

Reporting Guidelines

To help investigate your report, include as much relevant information as possible.

  • Issue Type. Specify whether you are reporting a bug or an exploit.
  • Description. Clearly explain what happened.
  • Expected Behavior. Explain what you expected to happen instead.
  • Steps to Reproduce. Provide the steps needed to reproduce the issue.
  • Additional Information. Include relevant logs, screenshots, or other useful details if available.

Submit Your Report

Once your report is ready, join the official Discord server and submit it through the appropriate channel.
